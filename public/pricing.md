# Pricing

Crosspost is free to use. There are no plans, tiers, or paid features.

## What you bring

- Your own X developer account (X's own pricing applies to their developer tiers).
- Your own tweet.app account.

Any costs from X or tweet.app are between you and those services. Crosspost itself charges nothing.

See the [setup guide](https://tweet.mikedemo.dev/docs/setup) to get started.
