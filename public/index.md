# Crosspost

Crosspost watches a tweet.app account and reposts new posts to X, automatically or after a quick review, using each person's own X developer keys.

## How it works

1. **Connect your tweet.app account** — sign in and choose the account to watch.
2. **Add your X developer keys** — your own X developer app, set to Read and Write.
3. **Pick your posting behavior** — automatic posting, or review-first where you approve each post before it goes out.

Crosspost never uses a shared account. You bring your own tweet.app account and your own X developer app, so everything posts as you and stays under your control.

## Pricing

Crosspost is free to use. You bring your own X developer account and your own tweet.app account; any costs from those services are between you and them.
