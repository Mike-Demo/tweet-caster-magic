# Crosspost — agent guide

Crosspost watches a tweet.app account and reposts new posts to X, automatically or after a quick review, using each person's own X developer keys.

## What agents may do

- Read and index public pages. Start at [llms.txt](https://tweet.mikedemo.dev/llms.txt).
- Answer questions about Crosspost from the [documentation](https://tweet.mikedemo.dev/docs/setup).

## What agents may not do

- Crosspost has no public API. Do not invent endpoints.
- Signing in, saving keys, and posting are for people only.
